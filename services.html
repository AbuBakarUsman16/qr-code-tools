Every generator on this site includes instant preview, a high resolution PNG download, and works entirely offline once the page has loaded, no internet connection is needed to generate a code after that point. There are no usage limits and no watermarks added to your downloads.

The offline column is worth a second look. A WiFi code has to work before the guest has internet, which is exactly why it cannot be a redirect based code.

Every tool is static by design

All six generators produce static codes, meaning the data lives inside the image rather than on a server. The practical consequences:

  • No expiry. A code printed today still works in ten years.
  • Nothing to switch off. There is no account, subscription or service that can lapse and take your printed codes with it.
  • No third party in the path. Nobody logs your scans, and the preview your scanner shows is your own address rather than a stranger's domain.
  • Works offline for WiFi, contact, text and SMS codes.

The tradeoff is that you cannot change a code's destination after printing, and you get no built in scan counter. Our guide on static versus dynamic codes explains when that tradeoff is worth making, and how to get an editable destination without a subscription.

Privacy: nothing leaves your browser

Every generator runs entirely as JavaScript in your own browser. When you type a WiFi password, a phone number or a contact card, that information is used to draw an image on your screen and then discarded. It is never transmitted to us, never stored and never logged. There is no account to create and nothing to delete afterwards. The full detail is in our privacy policy.

Who uses these tools

The generators were built for specific, repeated problems rather than as a general utility set. The patterns we see most:

  • Short term rental hosts printing a WiFi code for the welcome card, plus a text code for checkout instructions that works without internet.
  • Cafés and restaurants putting a WiFi code on table cards and a review link at the till.
  • Hotels replacing the in room compendium with a directory link and cutting WiFi questions at the front desk.
  • Freelancers and sales teams printing a vCard code on the back of a business card so details land in the phone rather than a pocket.
  • Tradespeople putting an SMS or email code on a van or job site board so a passerby can make contact without writing anything down.
  • Facilities and maintenance teams labelling equipment with text codes that hold asset numbers and service dates, readable in basements with no signal.

What we do not offer

Being direct about the limits, so you can tell quickly whether this is the right site:

  • No scan analytics. Static codes involve no server, so there is nothing counting scans. Point the code at your own page if you need numbers.
  • No editable destinations. A printed code cannot be repointed. Link to a page you control if the destination may change.
  • No styling without a warning attached. The designer does offer colours, a logo, dot styles and a frame, because people need them. It also decodes every change with a real scanner library and tells you when a choice has broken the code. What we will not do is hand you a pretty code that does not scan.

If you genuinely need tracking or editable links, a paid dynamic service is the honest answer, and our comparison guide explains what to weigh up first.

Common questions about the tools

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup, no email required and no login on any tool. Open the page, fill in the fields, download the PNG.

Is there a limit on how many codes I can make?

No. There is no daily cap, no watermark and no restriction on commercial use.

Will the codes still work in a few years?

Yes. Because the data sits inside the image rather than on a server, a code printed today resolves identically in a decade. Nothing on our side can expire it.
