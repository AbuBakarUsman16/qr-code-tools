Who These Services Are For

Small business owners use our URL and WiFi generators to connect customers to menus, product pages, and guest networks. Freelancers and sales teams use the vCard generator as a paperless business card. Event organizers and educators use the text generator for quick, scannable instructions or notes.

What's Included With Every Tool

Every generator on this site includes instant preview, a high-resolution PNG download, and works entirely offline once the page has loaded — no internet connection is needed to generate a code after that point. There are no usage limits and no watermarks added to your downloads.
