How WiFi QR Codes Work

Modern phone cameras can read a special QR format that encodes your network name, password, and security type. When a guest scans it, their phone offers to join the network directly — no manual typing, no reading a password off a sticky note.

Where to Use It

Print it on a card for your guest room, frame it near your café counter, or include it in a welcome packet for short-term rentals. If you change your WiFi password later, just generate a new code with the updated details.
