How to Create a URL QR Code

Enter the full web address you want people to visit, including "https://" at the start, then click Generate. The QR code updates instantly and can be downloaded as a PNG image for use on websites, posters, packaging, or business cards.

Common Uses for URL QR Codes

Restaurants use them to link to digital menus, marketers use them on flyers to track campaign traffic, and businesses print them on packaging to direct customers to product pages or reviews. Because the destination is a live link, you can update the page content later without needing to reprint the QR code.
