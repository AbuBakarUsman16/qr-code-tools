What Is a vCard QR Code?

A vCard QR code stores a digital business card in a standard contact-card format (vCard). When someone scans it with their phone camera, their device recognizes the contact info and offers to save it directly — no app needed on iOS or Android.

How to Use It

Fill in as many fields as you'd like included on the card, then click Generate. Download the resulting QR code and add it to your email signature, printed business card, or event badge so people can save your details with a single scan instead of typing them manually.
