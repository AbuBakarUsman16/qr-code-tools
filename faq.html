Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our QR code generators and how to use them.

Are these QR code generators free to use?

Yes. Every tool on QRTools is completely free, with no signup, no watermark, and no usage limits.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. The data is embedded directly into the QR code image itself rather than pointing to a redirect link, so the codes work indefinitely and do not expire.

Is my data safe when I generate a QR code here?

Yes. All QR codes are generated locally in your browser. Nothing you type, including WiFi passwords or contact details, is sent to a server or stored anywhere.

Can I use these QR codes for print materials?

Yes. Download the PNG and use it on business cards, posters, flyers, or packaging. For large prints, avoid shrinking the code below about 2cm per side to keep it reliably scannable.

Why isn't my QR code scanning?

This is usually caused by low print contrast (light gray on white, for example), the code being printed too small, or too much text encoded into a text QR code, which increases pattern density and makes it harder to read. Try increasing the print size or shortening the input text.

Do I need an app to scan these QR codes?

No. Modern iOS and Android phones can scan QR codes directly from the built-in camera app without installing anything extra.

Can I edit a QR code after generating it?

Not directly — QR codes encode fixed data. If you need to change the content (like a new WiFi password), simply generate a new code with the updated details and replace the old one.
