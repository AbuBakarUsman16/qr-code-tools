Why Use These QR Code Generators?

QR codes have become the fastest way to move information from a screen or printed page directly into someone's phone. Whether you're sharing a business card at a networking event, giving guests quick access to your home WiFi, or linking customers to your website from a poster, a QR code removes the friction of typing anything by hand.

All of the tools on this site run entirely in your browser. That means your contact details, WiFi password, or text never gets uploaded to a server — the QR code is generated locally on your device, which is faster and more private than most generators you'll find elsewhere.

How to Choose the Right QR Code Type

If you want people to visit a webpage, use the URL generator. If you're handing out a digital business card, the vCard generator saves your full contact info straight into someone's phone contacts with one scan. For cafés, offices, or events where guests need internet access without reading out a password, the WiFi generator is the quickest option. And for anything else — a short message, an address, a code — the plain text generator handles it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these QR code generators really free? Yes, all tools on this site are completely free to use with no account or signup needed.

Do the QR codes expire? No. Because the data is embedded directly into the QR code image itself (not a redirect link), the codes never expire and will keep working indefinitely.

Can I use these QR codes for print? Yes, download the PNG and use it on business cards, posters, flyers, or packaging. For large prints, generate at a higher size for best scan reliability.
