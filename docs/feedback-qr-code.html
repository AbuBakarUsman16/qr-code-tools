Modes and fields

Google review Default

Your Google Place ID builds a link to the write a review box. Or paste a review link you already have uses your link instead. If both are filled, your own link wins.

A form Optional

Any form address: Google Forms, Typeform or a page on your site. https:// is added if missing.

Email Optional

Where feedback should go, a subject (Feedback by default) and an optional opening line. Makes a mailto: link.

What it does not do

  • It cannot find your Place ID for you; use Google's Place ID Finder.
  • It collects no feedback itself and shows you no results.
