About QRTools

A small set of QR code tools built to be fast, free, and respectful of your data.

Why We Built This

Most QR code generators online ask you to sign up, watch for hidden fees, or route your data through a server before handing you an image. We wanted something simpler: open a page, fill in your details, get a QR code, download it — nothing else in the way.

How It Works

Every QR code on this site is generated directly in your browser. Whether you're building a vCard, a WiFi login code, or a link to your website, the data you type never leaves your device or touches a server. The QR code you see on screen is created locally and stays that way until you download it.

What We Offer

QRTools currently includes generators for website URLs, digital business cards (vCard), WiFi network sharing, and plain text. Each tool is built for one job and does it well, rather than trying to be an all-in-one dashboard. See the full list on our Services page.

Our Approach

No account required. No watermark on your QR codes. No expiration dates. We keep the site simple so it stays fast, and we keep it free because a QR code generator shouldn't come with a paywall.
