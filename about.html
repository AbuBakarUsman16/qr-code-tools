About QRotto

A small set of QR code tools built to be fast, free, and respectful of your data.

Why We Built This

Most QR code generators online ask you to sign up, watch for hidden fees, or route your data through a server before handing you an image. We wanted something simpler: open a page, fill in your details, get a QR code, download it — nothing else in the way.

How It Works

Every QR code on this site is generated directly in your browser. Whether you're building a vCard, a WiFi login code, or a link to your website, the data you type never leaves your device or touches a server. The QR code you see on screen is created locally and stays that way until you download it.

What We Offer

QRotto currently includes generators for website URLs, digital business cards (vCard), WiFi network sharing, and plain text. Each tool is built for one job and does it well, rather than trying to be an all-in-one dashboard. See the full list on our Services page.

Our Approach

No account required. No watermark on your QR codes. No expiration dates. We keep the site simple so it stays fast, and we keep it free because a QR code generator shouldn't come with a paywall.

Questions?

Check our FAQ page for common questions about how the tools work and how to use the QR codes you generate.

Who runs this site

QRotto is built and maintained by Abu Bakar Usman. The tools here started as something I needed myself — a WiFi code for a guest room that did not require signing up to a service or trusting a stranger's server with a password — and grew from there as other people asked for variations.

Everything on the site is written and tested by one person rather than assembled from templates. When a guide says a code should be printed at a certain size, or that a particular test is the only meaningful one, that comes from checking it on real phones and real print rather than repeating what other sites say.

How we test

Every generator on this site is checked the same way before it goes live:

  • The generated code is decoded programmatically to confirm the data it contains matches the input exactly.
  • It is scanned with both an iPhone and an Android device using only the built-in camera app — no third-party scanner.
  • WiFi codes are tested on a device that has been made to forget the network, since a connected phone will appear to succeed regardless.
  • Codes are printed and scanned at realistic sizes and distances before any sizing advice is published.

What we do not do

  • No accounts. Nothing here requires signing up, and there is no account for us to lose control of.
  • No dynamic codes. Every code is static, meaning your data lives in the image rather than on our server. Nothing we do can make your printed codes stop working later.
  • No data collection through the tools. The generators run entirely in your browser. Passwords, contact details and messages never reach us — see the privacy policy.
  • No watermarks or usage limits. Codes you generate are yours, including for commercial use.

How the site is funded

The tools are free and stay free. The site is supported by advertising, which is why you will see ad slots on some pages. Ads are kept away from the generate and download buttons deliberately — a tool that tricks you into tapping an advert is a broken tool.

Corrections and feedback

If something here is wrong, or a tool does not behave as described, tell us through the contact page and it will be fixed. Most of the tools on this site exist because someone asked for them.
