Privacy policy: Qrotto SEO Bar

Last updated 21 October 2026. Qrotto SEO Bar is a free Chrome extension that shows Domain Rating, domain age and keyword ideas on Google search results. This page explains what it does with data.

What the extension stores

  • Your Ahrefs API key. When you paste your own key, it is saved in your browser with Chrome's storage. It stays on your device and is sent only to Ahrefs, to fetch Domain Rating. It is never sent to Qrotto.
  • A local cache of Domain Rating and domain age results, kept on your device for up to 7 days so the extension is faster. It never leaves your browser.

What the extension sends, and to whom

When you search on Google, the extension may send these requests directly from your browser:

  • the domains shown in your results to Ahrefs (api.ahrefs.com) to get Domain Rating, using your own key;
  • the same domains to RDAP (rdap.org) to look up when each domain was registered;
  • your search query to Google Autocomplete (suggestqueries.google.com) to fetch related keyword ideas.

These requests exist only to show you the features above. Each service handles the request under its own privacy policy. The extension sends no data to Qrotto, and adds no tracking, advertising or analytics.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, passwords, form data or browsing history.
  • It does not sell any data, and it shares nothing beyond the requests listed above.
  • It has no account, no login and no server of its own.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your API key and the local cache.
  • Access to api.ahrefs.com, rdap.org and suggestqueries.google.com: to fetch the metrics and keyword ideas described above.

Third party data

Domain Rating by Ahrefs, used under the Ahrefs Domain Rating License. Keyword ideas come from Google Autocomplete.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use our contact page. If the extension changes what it sends or stores, this page will be updated first.

This policy covers the extension only. For the qrotto.com website, see the website privacy policy.
