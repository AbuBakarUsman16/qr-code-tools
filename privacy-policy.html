Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

QRotto ("we", "us", "this site") provides free QR code generation tools. This policy explains what information is collected when you use this website and how it is handled.

Data You Enter Into Our Tools

All QR codes on this site are generated entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript. The information you type into any generator — including names, phone numbers, email addresses, WiFi passwords, URLs, and text — is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never logged. It exists only in your browser's memory while the page is open and is discarded when you close or reload the page.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you visit. This information is used only to monitor site performance and security.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. However, third-party services described below may place cookies on your device.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads on this site. You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as which pages are most visited. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

Third-Party Resources

This site loads a QR code generation library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). When your browser requests this file, the CDN provider may log standard request information such as your IP address.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data submitted through our tools, there is no personal data for us to retrieve, correct, or delete. For data handled by third parties such as Google, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through our Contact page.
